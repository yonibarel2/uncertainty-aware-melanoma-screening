# Deep Learning Course – Project Guidelines

> Faithful English translation of the official course guidelines (original in Hebrew).

## Introduction and Goals

The course project is intended to provide practical, systematic and in-depth experience in developing, implementing and analyzing advanced deep learning methods. The project combines theoretical understanding, experiment design, code implementation and analysis of results. The project is a central component of the course (**80% of the final grade**), and accordingly it is expected to meet high academic standards of clarity, accuracy, depth and methodology.

## Teamwork

The project is carried out in pairs. In exceptional cases, and as needed, work in triples will also be allowed with prior approval. Team composition is determined by the students themselves. Each team member is required to make a substantial contribution to the project. **The final report must include a detailed description of the division of work between the team members.** Unbalanced contribution or insufficient involvement may affect the individual evaluation. Note that the level of expectations from a project carried out by three students is higher than that of a project carried out by two students, both in terms of the scope of work and in terms of the depth of the analysis and the contribution.

## Project Types

The project may belong to one or more of the following categories:

- **Applied project:** applying deep learning methods to solve a real problem (e.g., in computer vision, natural language processing, medicine, finance, etc.).
- **Algorithmic project:** developing a new method or substantially improving an existing method.
- **Theoretical project:** mathematical analysis or proving properties of algorithms.

It is possible, and even recommended, to combine categories, for example developing a new method and demonstrating it on an applied problem.

## Topic Selection and Literature Review

Choosing the topic is a critical stage. You should choose a topic of personal interest, scientific or applied relevance, and potential for depth. It is recommended to carry out an up-to-date literature review and to use academic search engines. A high-level project will be one that approaches the quality of publishable research.

## Use of Existing Models

Using existing models without training, adaptation or an additional methodological contribution does not meet the course requirements. **A substantial component of training a deep learning model is required.**

## Technical and Methodological Requirements

The project will be implemented in **PyTorch**. Students are expected to fill knowledge gaps independently, in particular to acquire proficiency in working with PyTorch, and to make use of the tools and working practices accepted in the field.

A systematic experiment design is required, including:

- Definition of a clear baseline for comparison
- Selection of appropriate evaluation metrics
- Comparison between methods/models
- In-depth analysis of the results

## Project Proposal

A PDF document of **300–500 words** must be submitted, including:

- Project title
- Full names of the team members
- Project category (e.g., Computer Vision, NLP, Speech recognition, Healthcare, Finance, or other)

The body of the proposal will include:

- A clear definition of the problem
- A description of the main challenges
- A description of the data
- A description of the proposed method
- Reference to existing work
- Details of planned improvements or innovations

The purpose of the proposal is to ensure there is a reasonable and well-founded work plan.

## Final Report

The final report will be submitted with a length of **up to five pages**, and will present a comprehensive description of the project.

The report will include:

- Motivation and problem definition
- Review of relevant work
- Description of the data
- Details of the models and hyperparameters
- Presentation of the results
- Analysis of the results
- Discussion of insights, limitations and future directions
- List of references

In addition, it must include:

- Details of the contribution of each team member
- A link to the code repository (e.g., GitHub)

The code should be organized, readable and documented in a way that allows understanding and reproduction. **Reproducibility of the results is important. Clear run instructions must be included.**

## Project Presentation

The project presentation is a significant component of the grade. The presentation lasts **15 minutes**. Make sure to stay within the time frame.

The presentation will include:

- Presentation of the motivation and the problem
- A brief review of relevant work
- Description of the method
- Details of the experiments
- Presentation of the results
- Analysis of the results
- Summary and insights

Make sure of:

- Clarity and logical organization
- Readable, uncluttered slides
- Proper use of graphs, tables and diagrams
- Avoiding irrelevant technical details

All team members are expected to participate in the presentation and demonstrate full command of the material, including the ability to answer questions professionally.

## Evaluation Criteria

The evaluation of the project will be based on the following criteria:

- **Technical quality:** methodological correctness, level of implementation and experimental depth.
- **Novelty:** originality of the idea or the contribution.
- **Significance:** importance of the problem and potential for impact.
- **Insights:** depth of the analysis and understanding of the results.
- **Report and presentation quality:** clarity, organization and scientific communication ability.

## Expectations and Standards

A good-level project is characterized by a correct and well-founded application of existing methods. An excellent-level project includes an original contribution, in-depth analysis, and meaningful insights derived from the experiments.

## Summary

The success of the project depends on the right choice of topic, systematic planning, careful implementation and the ability for critical analysis. Students are expected to present work at a high academic level, with attention to clarity, accuracy and depth.
